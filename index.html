Machine learning research · University of Bristol

Length generalisation in Transformer sequence models

Designed and executed 45 controlled multi-seed experiments across three algorithmic tasks and five positional encodings, using PyTorch and AWS GPU infrastructure to evaluate extrapolation at sequence lengths up to 1,024.

  • Redesigned a trivial copy benchmark as delayed copy to isolate long-range memory
  • Observed exact-match accuracy fall from 100% at the trained length to 0.8% at the first unseen length in preliminary addition experiments
  • Built an evidence-based failure taxonomy covering underfitting, seed instability, extrapolation collapse, and position-dependent errors
  • Evaluated mixed-length training, curriculum learning, and randomised padding using task-specific diagnostics

Socio-economic analysis · England & Wales

Heating deprivation and health outcomes

Harmonised 2011 and 2021 census data across local authorities and used Bayesian Ridge regression to impute missing values.

  • Identified private rental tenure as the strongest predictor of heating deprivation, with a standardised coefficient of 1.43 and a 95% confidence interval of [1.34, 1.52]
  • Found higher rates of heating deprivation among people aged 65 and over
  • Built interactive Tableau dashboards and geographic maps comparing heating access and health outcomes between 2011 and 2021
